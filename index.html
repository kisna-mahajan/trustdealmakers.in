Industrial asset monetisation is not simply about disposing of surplus equipment. It is about recovering maximum commercial value from productive industrial assets by identifying organisations that can continue using them efficiently.

A rolling mill replaced in one country may become the foundation of a growing manufacturing operation elsewhere. By connecting these opportunities, Trust DealMakers creates value for both sellers and buyers.

About Trust DealMakers

What is Trust DealMakers?

Trust DealMakers is a cross-border representation and market access company that helps manufacturers, technology providers, healthcare organisations, industrial enterprises, and international buyers build trusted commercial relationships between India and global markets. Rather than acting as a conventional trading company, we focus on market representation, business development, strategic sourcing, commercial partnerships, and long-term relationship building.

Are you a trading company?

No — not in the traditional sense. While we facilitate international trade where appropriate, our primary role is to help organisations develop markets, identify business partners, represent their commercial interests, source products and technologies, and build sustainable business relationships.

Are you consultants?

We provide strategic guidance where required, but our work extends beyond advisory services. We actively support market access, partner identification, supplier evaluation, commercial representation, negotiations, and business development.

India Market Entry

Can you help overseas manufacturers enter the Indian market?

Yes. We help overseas manufacturers understand the Indian market, identify customers, distributors, EPC companies, consultants, procurement teams, and strategic partners while providing structured commercial representation.

Which industries do you focus on?

Our immediate focus includes Precision Machinery, Machine Tools, Industrial Automation, Robotics, Aerospace Manufacturing Technologies, Defence Manufacturing Technologies, Steel & Metals, Medical Devices, Hospital Equipment, Semiconductor Manufacturing Ecosystems, Advanced Manufacturing, and Industrial Engineering.

Do you work with small and medium-sized manufacturers?

Yes. We work with businesses of different sizes where there is a strong strategic fit and long-term partnership potential.

India Buying Office

What is an India Buying Office?

An India Buying Office represents international organisations sourcing products, equipment, technologies, or services from Indian manufacturers. We help identify suppliers, evaluate capabilities, coordinate commercial discussions, and support sourcing decisions.

Can you identify OEM manufacturers?

Yes. We can help identify qualified manufacturers for OEM production, contract manufacturing, private-label manufacturing, and industrial sourcing assignments.

Do you conduct supplier evaluations?

Where appropriate, we assist with supplier assessment, capability review, commercial evaluation, and coordination with prospective manufacturing partners.

International Representation

Can Trust DealMakers represent our company in India?

Yes. Subject to strategic fit, we work with overseas manufacturers and technology companies seeking structured market development and commercial representation in India.

Do you represent competing companies?

Our representation approach depends on the nature of the assignment. We seek to avoid conflicts of interest and discuss exclusivity where appropriate.

Can you help us identify distributors and channel partners?

Yes. We assist in identifying potential distributors, dealers, business partners, and industrial customers aligned with your products and market objectives.

Technology Partnerships & Healthcare
